Ticket Pocket Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Ticket Pocket keeps your trips, plans, expenses and travel documents in one place and lets you share trips with the people you travel with. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. In short: we only keep what the app needs to work, we never sell it, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Data we keep

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple we receive an identifier for your Apple Account, your name and an email address (which can be a private relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”). You can change your name in the app.
  • What you add. Trips, cities, plans, expenses and how they are split, travel documents (for example a passport's number and expiration date) and the files you attach: PDFs, photos and Wallet passes.

We don't collect your device location, contacts, usage analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.

How it is used

Only to provide the app's features: syncing your data between your devices and with the people you share a trip with. Reading a reservation from a PDF or photo happens on your device with Apple's on-device models; the extracted text is not sent to us.

Who can see it

  • Trips are visible only to their members: you and the people who join through an invite link you share.
  • Documents are private. If you turn on “Visible to My Companions” for a document, members of trips you share can see it.
  • We don't sell or rent your data and don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and file storage, in the United States. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and access is restricted per trip and per person.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, maps and place search (MapKit) and weather (WeatherKit). To show the weather, the app sends a city's location and dates to Apple; to search places, it sends what you type. See Apple's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while you have an account. You can delete files, documents and trips at any time, and you can delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete Account. That permanently deletes your profile, your documents, the trips you created (for everyone in them) and their files, and revokes the app's access to your Apple Account. In trips created by someone else, your name stays on the expenses you shared so their balances remain correct.

Children

Ticket Pocket is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app and delete it as described above. For any other request about your data, write to sankurlabs@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
