Ticket Pocket Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to sankurlabs@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version if something isn't working.

How do I share a trip?

Open the trip, tap More (…) → Invite and send the link. When the other person opens it with Ticket Pocket installed and signs in, they join the trip. Only the person who created the trip can invite others.

Can I use it without a connection?

Yes. Everything you've opened is saved on your device, so you can read your itinerary, documents and attachments offline. Changes need a connection.

How do I import a reservation?

In a trip, tap Add (+), choose Import Reservation from Files or from Photos and pick a PDF, photo or Wallet pass, or share it to Ticket Pocket from Mail, Files or Photos. On devices with Apple Intelligence the details are filled in for you; otherwise you can enter them yourself.

Who can see my documents?

Only you, unless you turn on “Visible to My Companions” for a document.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm with Apple. See the Privacy Policy for what gets deleted.
